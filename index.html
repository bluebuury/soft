who we are

hi, we're soft boy

we're a little taiyaki and soft serve shop in fairfax, virginia. every taiyaki is made to order from scratch: crispy on the outside, chewy mochi inside, with locally-sourced ingredients and fillings we obsess over.

our soft serve is ultra rich and creamy, with asian-inspired flavors and that dense, silky consistency you find in japan. come for the fish, stay for the swirl.
